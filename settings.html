PlexDeck settings

Skip intros and credits

PlexDeck presses Plex's own Skip Intro / Skip Credits button once it has been on screen this long, so you still get a few seconds to watch. Your server needs intro/credit markers (Plex makes them for most TV).

Quality, subtitles, audio language and auto-play next episode are in Plex's own settings.

Theme

Look

With a Windows contrast theme on, Windows recolours every page and the volume and seek sliders can disappear. Leave this on to keep Plex's own colours; turn it off to use your contrast theme.

PC / TV mode

TV mode is a controller-first layout like Plex's TV apps. Switch any time with the PC | TV buttons in the title bar or F10.

Picture & sound (native player)

Subtitles & languages (native player)

Picture & sound settings take effect the next time a video starts. Styled (ASS) subtitles keep their own look.

Window

With the tray on, ✕ hides the window and music keeps playing. Click the tray icon to come back; right-click it for Play/Pause, skip and Quit.

Shortcuts: Ctrl + / − / 0 text size · F11 full screen · Alt ← / → back / forward · F5 reload · Ctrl , these settings.

Navigation

Arrow keys move an orange ring between posters and buttons; Enter opens. In the player the arrows seek and change volume as usual.

Media keys and most remotes (Play/Pause, Next, Previous, Volume) work out of the box.

What PlexDeck shows

Libraries, Home rows, pinned libraries and their order are managed in Plex itself (hover a library → ⋯ → Pin / Manage).

PlexDeck

An unofficial Windows desktop app for Plex, by Farathim. It runs the official Plex web app and adds the extras above.

Your settings from the older PlexPlayer were imported on first start.

Back up your settings

Save PlexDeck's settings (theme, player, subtitles, controller, TV mode) to a file, or load them on another PC. Your Plex sign-in and server address are never in the file.
